The Community Table

This is where visitors argue, agree, and swap food stories — out in the open.

⚠ Comments aren't live yet — quick setup for the site owner

Comments on this page run on Cusdis (free, lightweight, works on static hosting — and visitors can post with no account at all, even anonymously). To go live:

  1. Sign up at cusdis.com and add the website isukiro.github.io/food-rankings.
  2. In the Cusdis dashboard, copy the App ID (anonymous/guest commenting is on by default).
  3. Hand the App ID to the assistant — the embed below goes live with it.

Until then, the discussion area shows a "coming soon" card. New comments wait for your approval in the Cusdis dashboard, so no spam surprises.

Two things look similar on this site but do very different jobs: the comments below are public discussion with other visitors — no account needed, post as a guest or anonymously, and everything you write is visible to everyone once approved. The star ratings you see on rankings and food pages are the opposite: they are your personal scores, stored only in your own browser, never uploaded, never shared.

Debate the rankings, recommend hidden gems, or correct us — politely. Taste is personal, and the table is big enough for all of it.
